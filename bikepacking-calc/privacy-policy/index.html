Privacy Policy for Bikepacking Calc

Effective date: 22 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bikepacking Calc (“the App”) handles information. I am Vadim Turko, the independent developer of the App (“I”, “me”).

Bikepacking Calc is a planning tool for bikepacking setups. You can use it fully without an account and fully offline. Your gear, bicycles, setups, and preferences stay on your device. The only information I send off the device is crash and error reports through Firebase Crashlytics.

If you have questions, contact me at contact.vodemn@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the content you create and a few display preferences locally (SQLite database and on-device preferences). This can include:

  • Bicycles, mounting points, categories, gear items, and trip setups (names, weights, quantities, packed state, and similar planning data you enter)
  • Display preferences such as theme (light / dark / system) and weight units (metric / imperial)

This information is not sent to me. It is not backed up to my servers because I do not operate an app backend for user data. The operating system may include the App’s local files in its own device backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) according to your device settings; those backups are controlled by Apple or Google, not by me.

Uninstalling the App deletes this local data from the device. I cannot recover it.

3. Information I send off the device (crash reports)

To keep the App stable I use Google Firebase Crashlytics. Crash reporting is enabled in release builds and cannot be turned off in the App.

When the App crashes or records an unexpected error, Crashlytics may send Google information such as:

  • Stack traces and error messages
  • App version and build
  • Device model, OS version, and similar technical state (for example available memory)
  • An installation or app-instance identifier used to group crashes
  • The approximate time of the crash

Crash reports can include fragments of diagnostic context from the failing code. I do not intentionally attach your names, gear lists, or other planning content to crash reports. I do not set a personal user identifier (such as an email or account id) in Crashlytics, because the App has no accounts.

Google processes this data as my service provider. Google’s handling of Crashlytics data is described in Google’s Firebase privacy documentation and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Why this is required. I use crash reports only to find and fix bugs. Because the App has no account and no settings toggle for this, using the App means crash reports may be sent when a failure occurs and a network connection is available. If you do not want crash reports sent, do not install or use the App.

4. What I do not collect

I do not collect:

  • Name, email, or other contact details through the App (unless you email me yourself)
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or calendar access
  • Advertising identifiers for ads or profiling
  • Usage analytics (screens viewed, feature funnels, and similar)

The App does not currently request sensitive device permissions beyond what the OS and Crashlytics need to run and to send crash reports (network access).

5. How I use information

  • On-device data is used only to provide the App’s features to you on that device.
  • Crash reports are used to diagnose failures, improve reliability, and prioritize fixes.

I do not sell your information. I do not use it for advertising. I do not share on-device planning data with anyone because I never receive it.

6. Payments and planned Pro mode

The App does not currently offer in-app purchases, subscriptions, or a paid Pro mode.

If I add Pro (or similar paid features):

  • Payment would be processed by Apple (App Store) and/or Google (Play Store), not by me. I would not receive your full payment card number.
  • I would store on the device (and, if the store provides it, receive) only what is needed to unlock the feature, such as purchase or subscription status and a store transaction identifier.
  • Those platforms have their own privacy policies. I would update this document before charging for Pro.

Until then, nothing in this policy should be read as saying that I process payment data today.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through crash reports or by contacting me, email me and I will delete what I control.

8. Data retention

  • On-device data remains until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Crash reports are retained in Firebase according to Google’s Crashlytics retention settings (typically on the order of months, not indefinitely). I keep them only as long as they are useful for debugging.

9. Your choices and rights

Because I do not operate user accounts:

  • You can delete local App data by clearing the App’s storage (Android) or deleting the App.
  • You can stop future crash reports by uninstalling the App.
  • You cannot opt out of Crashlytics while the App remains installed.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data I hold, or to object to processing. For me, that mainly covers crash reports and any email you send. Contact contact.vodemn@gmail.com and I will respond as required by applicable law. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If I process crash reports, I do so to operate and improve a reliable App (legitimate interests, and/or as needed to provide the service you chose to install). Email you send me is processed to respond to you.

10. International transfers

I am an independent developer. Google may process Crashlytics data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google is responsible for the safeguards that apply to those transfers, as described in its privacy documentation.
